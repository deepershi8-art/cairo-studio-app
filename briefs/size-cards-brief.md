# Developer brief — Page 1 "Volume of air" size cards (supplied by Deeper Shi, 15 Oct 2026)

*Saved verbatim as supplied (essentials).*

1. Objective: make the difference between WITHOUT (constrained smaller room) and WITH (flagship live room) immediately audible and visually understandable. Listener hears the acoustic phenomenon itself, not two reverb presets. Per card: identify cause; demonstrate audibly in WITHOUT; change the relevant room property in WITH; listener hears the change; show direct sound, reflections, combined waveform, frequency response or decay envelope explaining it. Sound and graphic tell the same story. Rule: waves don't stop overlapping in a larger room; larger dimensions change reflection paths, modal frequencies, timing, distribution — demonstrate the specific change, never claim all interference disappears.
2. Reference rooms: WITHOUT 6.0×5.0×2.7 m (30 m², 81 m³). WITH 10.0×9.5×5.2 m (95 m², 494 m³). Card 8 also 60 m² alternative 7.5×8.0×3.2 m (192 m³). c=343 m/s. Axial modes f_n=nc/(2L); reflection delays from actual source/mic/boundary geometry (image-source method). Measured IRs when appropriate, else explicitly labelled synthetic models from the stated geometry. Don't invent measurements of the future studio.
3. Every card: two selectable states WITHOUT/WITH. Same source recording, performance, timing, initial level; change only the acoustic variable (unless source position change is the concept). Build signal from direct sound + geometrically justified early reflections + diffuse/synthetic late reverb + combined mic output. Separate visuals for direct, individual reflections, combined output; label displays ('Direct signal', 'Reflected signals', 'Combined signal', 'Illustrative frequency response', 'Decay envelope'). Common time axis; animate actual calculated delays or derive animation from rendered audio. No generic bouncing particles, no sound visibly travelling to ceiling and back, no arbitrary waveform motion, whooshes or cinematic effects. Each state ≤ ~6 s incl. useful tail.
4. Card 1 Piano (volume & tall ceiling): cause = room dimensions change timing/distribution of reflections combining with direct piano. WITHOUT 30 m²/2.7 m: sustained chord with clear attack, direct + several early reflections from plausible piano/mic arrangement; audible comb filtering only if geometry gives it, defensible; plausible small-room decay (not excessively reverberant). WITH same chord/level in 95 m²/5.2 m, recalculated paths, larger-room response; don't remove all reflections or flatten. Hear: direct attack recognisable, reflection pattern/timbre/decay change. Visual: direct waveform, individual reflected waveforms, combined result; update delays; show room dimensions both states.
5. Card 2 Bass long wavelengths: low E 41.2 Hz, λ≈8.3 m. WITHOUT small-room modal frequencies; same sustained note through response for chosen source/mic positions; reinforcement/cancellation via room model, not arbitrary boost. WITH flagship geometry recalculated, same frequency and initial level. Don't shorten wavelength in visual; don't promise all resonance gone. Visual: 8.3 m ruler both states, room dimensions, frequency-response curve updating (label illustrative).
6. Card 3 Small-room modes boom & disappearance: WITHOUT small-room axial modes; controlled low tone or short sweep over one modal region; calculated/measured transfer function for specific source/listening positions; one identifiable peak and, if supported, one position-dependent null; if simplified filter, state it approximates a specific position. WITH proportionally comparable positions, recalculated modes; peak/null moves or changes severity per model; don't flatten with EQ; don't imply modes vanish. Visual: top-down room diagram, source & mic positions, pressure or frequency-response display animated during tone/sweep, calculated modal frequencies each state.
7. Card 4 Drums/cymbals ceiling-reflection timing: WITHOUT small room, dry snare then crash; ceiling path from drum & mic heights; direct transient + plausible ceiling reflection at calculated delay + suitable tail if justified. WITH flagship, same heights, recalculated delay & decay. No artificial brightening or level increase. Hear: different relationship between direct transient and early reflections, credible change in space. Visual: kit, overhead mic, ceiling height; direct transient and reflection marker on shared time axis, marker moves per calculated delay; decay envelope from rendered response.
8. Card 5 Violin, cello, oud, qanun spacing: WITHOUT small room, sources close as drawn; synchronised passage or timed entries; calculate direct path lengths & early reflections; preserve identities; no arbitrary chorus/widening/comb/random delays. WITH same passage in flagship, practical spacious arrangement, realistic mics; new arrival times & reflections; don't automatically widen everything. Hear: spatial presentation and direct/reflection interaction change; where supported, less cramped, easier to distinguish. Visual: four musicians & mic positions both rooms; stereo-stage graphic matching actual panning; animate each instrument envelope on entry; update reflection timing.
9. Card 6 The room answers: WITHOUT small-room model, short piano chord or snare; direct + early reflections + frequency-dependent decay (short only if appropriate to assumed treatment). WITH flagship model, same source, more developed reflection pattern & decay; longer RT60 not automatically better, not wet just because larger. Visual: initial transient and decay envelope driven by rendered response; if showing RT60, explain it is time for reverberant sound to decay by 60 dB, not until inaudible.
10. Card 7 Variable acoustics: BOTH states same 95 m² flagship room. WITHOUT = dry/heavily damped config; short pop source and separately if needed a string passage; same levels & positions. WITH = live config: same geometry, positions, performance; credible more reflective treatment (measured IR of corresponding state or defensible synthetic). Responses differ in actual reflection & decay, not by turning up an unrelated reverb. Visual: identical outline & dimensions; absorptive panels/curtains deployed in dry, alternate config in live; animate reflection & decay displays.
11. Card 8 Cost of cutting 95→60 m²: WITHOUT 7.5×8.0×3.2 (192 m³), modes & reflection paths, response appropriate to geometry, realistic ensemble layout; no deliberately unpleasant EQ/resonance/reverb. WITH 10.0×9.5×5.2 (494 m³), modes & paths, show extra placement options; comparable treatment assumptions, document differences. Don't imply small rooms always record poorly or software can't help. Visual: both plans at consistent scale, same symbols, calculated modal frequencies and reflection timings each, plus extra layout options in flagship.
12. Rules all cards: same sample/performance/timing/initial level between states; loudness-match output as closely as practical without removing intended differences (e.g. LUFS-style/RMS normalisation, document method); mono bass fundamentals centred; left-drawn sources on left, right on right; stereo IRs with intact L/R; widening/inter-channel delay only where modelled; true peaks below clipping with headroom; no arbitrary EQ, exaggerated bass, excessive reverb, gratuitous filtering, cinematic effects; clean stop/fade within target; repeated taps must not overlap or orphan nodes. Use Web Audio convolution, filters, gain, scheduling. Document per card: source, IR or synthetic response, processing parameters, calculated delays, loudness-matching method (put this in /workspace/cairo-studio-app/briefs/size-cards-implementation.md).
13. Acceptance: (1) cause identifiable by listening not caption; (2) WITHOUT demonstrates phenomenon audibly & plausibly; (3) WITH changes it for a defensible physical reason; (4) same performance & initial level both states; (5) visual shows the actual variable; (6) waveform/reflection timing/frequency curve/decay envelope agrees with rendered audio; (7) stereo image matches drawing; (8) no reliance on loudness or gratuitous processing; (9) each state within time limit with phenomenon audible; (10) simulated responses labelled illustrative, measured identified as measured.

Final: prioritise unmistakable, controlled cause and effect; don't force the larger room to win by removing all reflections, flattening responses or widening everything. Understandable, compelling, technically defensible.
